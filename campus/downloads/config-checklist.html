Configuration checklist

Five9 Lab study card. Numbers and names point at the sources. This is not a Five9 document.

  1. Outbound campaign
  2. Create skill groups.
  3. Create user profiles, then users, and put agents in the skills.
  4. Create contact fields and reconcile a list.
  5. Create dispositions. A final disposition stops the other numbers on that record for the campaign.
  6. Create a campaign profile. ANI and list behavior sit on the profile. One profile is assigned to the campaign.
  7. Create the campaign and choose preview, progressive, power, predictive, or manual touch on purpose.
  8. Inbound campaign
  9. Prompts exist before the script.
  10. The script uses the module names in the IVR guide.
  11. Skill Transfer names a skill, or you accept Default.
  12. The DNIS is on a campaign that is running. A schedule is how you close, not a stopped campaign you forget.
  13. Service level is the scoreboard you agreed to read.
  14. People and evidence
  15. Reason codes exist so Not Ready means something.
  16. The reporting role can generate a standard report.
  17. Call variables are flagged for reporting or they are not history.
  18. WEM, if you bought it, is a second product surface. Do not assume the dialer believes a schedule you never connected.
