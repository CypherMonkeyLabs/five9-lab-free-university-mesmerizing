Migration cutover card

Five9 Lab study card. Numbers and names point at the sources. This is not a Five9 document.

  1. Maintenance window is named, and a rollback DID is named.
  2. One DNIS moves. The campaign that owns it is running.
  3. A test call hits the skill you chose, not Default, unless Default was the plan.
  4. Recording notice and retention clock are known for that call.
  5. Abandon posture is the campaign field, not a number invented in a slide.
  6. Someone can stop the cut if 911, recording, or the test call fails.
  7. Skills exist, and the right users are in them.
  8. User profiles exist before user accounts.
  9. Contact fields, lists, and dispositions exist before the outbound campaign.
  10. Inbound and autodial have prompts and an IVR script.
  11. One campaign profile is assigned. ANI lives there, not in a rumor.
  12. Reporting role can open a standard report on a test call.
  13. DNC list and IVR DNC behavior are named.
  14. 911 locations match the rooms people sit in.
