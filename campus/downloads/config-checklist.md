# Configuration checklist

- Outbound campaign
- Create skill groups.
- Create user profiles, then users, and put agents in the skills.
- Create contact fields and reconcile a list.
- Create dispositions. A final disposition stops the other numbers on that record for the campaign.
- Create a campaign profile. ANI and list behavior sit on the profile. One profile is assigned to the campaign.
- Create the campaign and choose preview, progressive, power, predictive, or manual touch on purpose.
- Inbound campaign
- Prompts exist before the script.
- The script uses the module names in the IVR guide.
- Skill Transfer names a skill, or you accept Default.
- The DNIS is on a campaign that is running. A schedule is how you close, not a stopped campaign you forget.
- Service level is the scoreboard you agreed to read.
- People and evidence
- Reason codes exist so Not Ready means something.
- The reporting role can generate a standard report.
- Call variables are flagged for reporting or they are not history.
- WEM, if you bought it, is a second product surface. Do not assume the dialer believes a schedule you never connected.
