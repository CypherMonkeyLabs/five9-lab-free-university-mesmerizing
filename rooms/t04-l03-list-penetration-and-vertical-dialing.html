List penetration and vertical dialing

Lists, profiles, penetration versus vertical, resets, and the guardrails around time and detection.

Learn it in 5 minutes

HTMLMDPDFDOCX

Outbound lists · practice · 12 min full read

List penetration is the default. The dialer tries the first phone on every record, then the next phone on a later pass. Vertical dialing stays with one contact and moves across that contact's numbers before it moves on.

  • Walk both list modes on paper
  • Respect a final disposition

The community article's wording is easy to twist: it says the physical direction of list penetration is vertical, while the industry phrase vertical dialing means walking numbers across a record. Use the article's definitions, not the compass words. A final disposition stops every number on the record in either mode.

Walk six calls

Write the first six dials for penetration and for vertical on three records.

  1. Penetration: three number1 dials
  2. Vertical: one record's numbers
  3. Mark a final code and stop

The two lists of six are not the same.

  • Default list mode? List penetration.
  • Vertical in one sentence? Finish one record's numbers, then move on.
  • Compass words? Use the definitions, not 'vertical' as a direction.
  • Final disposition? Stops the whole record.
