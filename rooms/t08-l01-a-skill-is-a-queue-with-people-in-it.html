Skills

A skill is a queue with people in it

Queues, the awkward tab name, pools, levels, Default, and the scoreboard.

Learn it in 5 minutes

HTMLMDPDFDOCX

Skills routing · foundation · 12 min full read

A skill group holds users who have the agent or supervisor role. Campaigns point at one or more groups. The people, not the name, determine who can receive the contact.

  • Staff the skill
  • Set a service level you can explain

Service level, voicemail, greetings, and a message to the group are all on the skill topic. A skill with no members is a label.

Roster

Write a skill and three members.

  1. Name the skill
  2. Name who is excluded
  3. Name the service level as a target, not a priority

The roster is not the campaign.

  • Membership? Agent or supervisor.
  • Empty skill? No delivery.
  • Service level? On the skill.
  • Several skills per user? Yes.
