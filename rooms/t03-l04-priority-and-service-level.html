Priority and service level

DNIS, run state, scripts, overflow, schedules, and the pause between calls.

Learn it in 5 minutes

HTMLMDPDFDOCX

Inbound campaigns · practice · 12 min full read

The inbound topic says you can prioritize inbound traffic. Skill Transfer has a Priority tab in the community write-up. Skill groups themselves have service-level settings in the basic admin topic.

  • Separate priority from service level
  • Point at the screen for each

Priority is not the same number as a service-level target. One decides who gets the next agent when several calls wait. The other is how you measure whether the wait was acceptable. The public pages name both. They do not, in the text this lab read, give you a formula that mixes them.

Label the knobs

Write one sentence for priority and one for service level.

  1. Priority orders the offer
  2. Service level measures the wait
  3. You will not add the numbers

The sentences do not reuse the same verb.

  • Where is service level? On the skill group.
  • Where is IVR priority? Skill Transfer's priority tab.
  • Are they the same knob? No.
  • Who says inbound can be prioritized? The inbound campaign topic.
